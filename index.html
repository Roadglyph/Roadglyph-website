Hello, and welcome to RoadGlyph,

We act as publicists, marketers, and handlers for a select group of clients. We've worked with Directors of arts organisations, contemporary music ensembles, independent artists and producers, and healthcare providers. Most seek discretion in the handling of their accounts. However, our representatives would be happy to describe specifics of past work in meetings and presentations.

You have probably arrived with us via a reference. If so, please mention specifics when reaching out.

RoadGlyph offers tactical and/or strategic campaigns. Our work ranges between the simple set-up and day to day management of webpages and social-media profiles, through to full-blown publicity strategies for one-off projects, events, tours and/or longer term development. We also offer advice, training and guidance in preparation for press engagements across print, radio, podcasts and television.

You can reach us through our contact page.
